# Onboarding Procedures

**Northstar Technologies | Policy HR-005 | Version 1.0**

> **Fictional training document.** Northstar Technologies, its contacts, systems, and policies are invented for RAG demonstration. This document does not replace applicable law, plan documents, or written agreements.

## 1. Ownership and preparation

### 1.1 Purpose

This procedure defines a consistent new-hire experience. People Operations coordinates employment records; managers coordinate role readiness; IT provisions approved technology and access.

### 1.2 Before start date

People Operations sends a welcome packet, required employment forms, and People Portal instructions through approved channels. Managers assign an onboarding buddy and prepare a first-week agenda. IT records a device request and approved access list.

### 1.3 Sensitive documents

Submit identity, tax, and banking information only through the approved People Portal or authorized secure process. Do not email scans of identity documents to a manager or personal inbox.

## 2. Day-one checklist

### 2.1 People Operations

Verify onboarding tasks, work location, employment classification, payroll details, and required notices. Complete employment eligibility verification within applicable legal deadlines; ask People Operations about any accessibility or accommodation needs.

### 2.2 IT setup

Receive managed laptop and approved accessories, verify device inventory tag, enroll MFA, confirm password manager, and test email, collaboration tools, and VPN or approved secure access.

### 2.3 Manager introduction

Review role outcomes, team contacts, core hours, communication norms, hybrid schedule, and first 30-day goals. Introduce the onboarding buddy and explain how to ask for help.

### 2.4 Security orientation

Complete initial security training before receiving sensitive production access. Confirm how to report suspicious messages, lost devices, and accidental disclosures.

## 3. First week and first month

### 3.1 First week

Complete required policy acknowledgments, benefits enrollment orientation, payroll verification, and tool training. Managers review the first meaningful work assignment and expected deliverables.

### 3.2 Benefits enrollment

Eligible new hires generally have 30 calendar days from hire to submit benefit elections under BEN-001, subject to plan-specific rules. A manager cannot extend an enrollment deadline.

### 3.3 Thirty-day check-in

Manager and employee review onboarding blockers, access needs, training progress, and expectations. People Operations resolves any employment or benefits issues that require confidential handling.

### 3.4 Sixty- and ninety-day check-ins

Managers provide structured feedback and update development goals. These check-ins do not create a universal probationary period or override employment rights.

## 4. Access and exceptions

### 4.1 Least privilege

Managers request only access needed for the employee’s role. IT and application owners approve access according to SEC-001. Access to Restricted data requires additional justification and training.

### 4.2 Equipment delay

If a laptop is delayed, IT may issue a managed loaner. New hires must not use a personal or shared household computer for company data without written IT authorization.

### 4.3 Remote hires

People Operations verifies approved work location under HR-004; IT arranges secure shipment. Remote employees must follow the same security and timekeeping rules as office-based employees.

## 5. Completion and escalation

### 5.1 Completion record

The manager confirms role readiness; People Operations records policy acknowledgments and required onboarding forms; IT closes provisioning tickets only after access checks.

### 5.2 Example: missing MFA

A new hire cannot access email because MFA enrollment failed. They contact helpdesk@northstar.example, complete identity verification, and use the IT-002 recovery process; they do not borrow a colleague’s login.

### 5.3 Where to ask

Employment and benefits: people@northstar.example. Device or login: helpdesk@northstar.example. Security concerns: security@northstar.example. Payroll: payroll@northstar.example.
