# Equipment Policy

**Northstar Technologies | Policy IT-001 | Version 1.0**

> **Fictional training document.** Northstar Technologies, its contacts, systems, and policies are invented for RAG demonstration. This document does not replace applicable law, plan documents, or written agreements.

## 1. Equipment ownership

### 1.1 Standard issue

Northstar ordinarily provides one managed laptop, charger, and required security accessories to employees whose roles need them. Role-specific peripherals are approved by IT based on documented business need.

### 1.2 Ownership and inventory

Company-issued equipment remains Northstar property. IT records asset tag, serial number, assigned employee, issue date, and return status. Employees may not sell, lend, or transfer equipment without IT authorization.

### 1.3 Personal devices

Personal or shared devices are not approved for storing or processing company data unless IT issues a documented exception with required controls.

## 2. Requests and approvals

### 2.1 New-hire provisioning

Managers submit device and access needs through the IT Service Desk before the employee’s start date. IT selects standard hardware; exceptions require IT Operations approval.

### 2.2 Accessory requests

Request monitors, headsets, keyboards, and ergonomic accessories through IT Service Desk with business justification. Do not purchase first and assume reimbursement.

### 2.3 Purchase coordination

If IT authorizes an employee purchase, FIN-001 reimbursement thresholds and receipts still apply. A $280 monitor requires IT authorization and manager preapproval before purchase; Finance confirms eligibility.

### 2.4 Replacement cycle

IT evaluates standard laptops for replacement around the fourth year of service or sooner when security, warranty, or performance needs justify it. Replacement is not an automatic employee entitlement.

## 3. Care and security

### 3.1 Daily use

Keep devices physically secure, lock screens, install required updates, and use approved storage and collaboration tools. Do not disable encryption or endpoint controls.

### 3.2 Travel and remote work

Carry laptops in hand luggage where practical and avoid leaving them unattended in public or vehicles. Remote employees follow HR-004 workspace rules.

### 3.3 Damage and loss

Report lost, stolen, or damaged equipment immediately to IT Service Desk. Suspected theft or data exposure must also be reported immediately to Security under SEC-001. Do not attempt unauthorized repairs.

### 3.4 Personal use

Incidental personal use may be allowed if lawful and consistent with security rules, but company devices are primarily for work and remain subject to authorized administration and monitoring.

## 4. Support and return

### 4.1 Repair workflow

Open a ticket with asset tag, symptoms, location, and business impact. IT determines remote troubleshooting, repair, loaner, or replacement. Never ship a device to an unapproved vendor.

### 4.2 Offboarding

On separation or role change, follow IT return instructions and deadlines. Normally ship or return equipment within five business days after the final workday using the provided label or office drop-off process; applicable law controls any deductions.

### 4.3 Data handling

Do not wipe, sell, or copy company files before returning a device. IT performs approved retention and secure erasure; personal files on company devices may not be recoverable.

### 4.4 Examples

**My laptop broke during travel.** Report it to IT and request a managed loaner. **Can I give my old laptop to a teammate?** No, IT must reassign it. **Can I expense a desk chair?** Ask IT and Finance first; reimbursement is not automatic.
