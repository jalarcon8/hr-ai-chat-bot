# Leave of Absence Policy

**Northstar Technologies | Policy HR-006 | Version 1.0**

> **Fictional training document.** Northstar Technologies, its contacts, systems, and policies are invented for RAG demonstration. This document does not replace applicable law, plan documents, or written agreements.

## 1. Scope and principles

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy covers extended or protected absences, including medical, family, parental, bereavement, jury duty, military service, and discretionary personal leave. Eligibility and protections depend on law, work location, and individual circumstances.

### 1.2 Relationship to PTO

HR-002 covers ordinary accrued PTO. An absence may qualify for protected leave even when an employee has no PTO balance. People Operations determines when paid leave may run concurrently with protected leave, consistent with applicable law.

### 1.3 Privacy and nonretaliation

Employees should provide sensitive medical documentation to People Operations or an authorized administrator, not a line manager. Retaliation for requesting legally protected leave is prohibited.

## 2. Requesting leave

### 2.1 Planned leave

Notify People Operations and the manager as early as practicable, ordinarily at least 30 calendar days ahead when the need is foreseeable and the law permits. The manager receives scheduling information, not confidential diagnoses.

### 2.2 Unexpected leave

For emergencies, notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible and contact People Operations when able. The ordinary five-business-day PTO notice rule does not delay urgent or protected leave.

### 2.3 Documentation

People Operations explains eligibility, required forms, certification deadlines, benefits, and return-to-work steps in writing. Employees should respond within stated lawful deadlines; extensions or accommodations may be available.

### 2.4 Approval authority

People Operations determines statutory and company leave eligibility. Managers coordinate coverage but cannot deny a protected leave because of staffing preferences.

## 3. Leave categories

### 3.1 Medical and family leave

Eligible employees may qualify for job-protected leave under federal, state, or local laws. The applicable entitlement, measurement period, pay status, and documentation are determined case by case.

### 3.2 Parental leave

Northstar offers up to six weeks of company-paid bonding leave for eligible regular full-time employees after birth, adoption, or qualifying placement, subject to written plan terms and coordination with legally required benefits. People Operations confirms eligibility and concurrent leave rules before leave begins.

### 3.3 Bereavement

Regular full-time employees may request up to three paid workdays following the death of an immediate family member, with up to five days when significant travel is required and People Operations approves. Additional time may be covered by PTO, unpaid leave, or local law.

### 3.4 Jury duty and military service

Notify People Operations promptly after receiving a summons or orders. Pay, reinstatement, and benefit protections follow applicable law; do not assume PTO must be used.

### 3.5 Personal leave

Employees may request discretionary unpaid personal leave for circumstances not covered by protected leave. Approval is not guaranteed and requires manager and People Operations review.

## 4. Pay, benefits, and accrual

### 4.1 Pay during leave

Some leave is unpaid; other leave may be paid by Northstar, accrued PTO, insurance, or statutory programs. People Operations provides an individualized written designation and pay summary.

### 4.2 PTO accrual

Under the standard HR-002 plan, PTO accrues during paid working time and paid PTO but is generally suspended for unpaid leave days unless law or a written arrangement requires otherwise.

### 4.3 Holiday pay

Holiday pay during leave is determined under HR-003, the specific leave designation, and applicable law. Employees should not independently code company holidays.

### 4.4 Benefit continuation

Medical coverage, premium payments, and other benefits during leave depend on BEN-001, governing plan terms, and law. People Operations explains employee payment obligations.

## 5. Return and examples

### 5.1 Return-to-work coordination

Before the expected return, contact People Operations about required clearance, accommodations, scheduling, and system access. Medical clearance is requested only where lawful and appropriate.

### 5.2 Example: new parent

An employee expecting a child contacts People Operations in advance. People Operations evaluates statutory leave, the six-week company-paid bonding benefit, benefits continuation, and any requested PTO without assuming all periods add together.

### 5.3 Example: sudden illness

An employee unexpectedly needs extended medical leave. They notify the manager when practicable, contact People Operations, and receive individualized instructions; the manager should not demand a diagnosis.

### 5.4 Contact

Send leave and accommodation questions to people@northstar.example. Report suspected retaliation or discrimination through ethics@northstar.example.
