# Workplace Conduct Policy

**Northstar Technologies | Policy HR-007 | Version 1.0**

> **Fictional training document.** Northstar Technologies, its contacts, systems, and policies are invented for RAG demonstration. This document does not replace applicable law, plan documents, or written agreements.

## 1. Standards and scope

### 1.1 Respectful conduct

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, contractors, and visitors professionally in offices, remote meetings, work chat, travel, and company-sponsored events.

### 1.2 Harassment and discrimination

Northstar prohibits unlawful harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, and abusive conduct. Protected characteristics and legal protections vary by jurisdiction; People Operations applies the governing law.

### 1.3 Examples

Examples include repeated derogatory comments, unwelcome sexual remarks, intimidation, exclusionary behavior tied to protected status, and abusive messages in collaboration tools. An isolated incident may still require review.

## 2. Conflicts, gifts, and integrity

### 2.1 Conflicts of interest

Disclose personal, financial, or family interests that could affect purchasing, hiring, vendor selection, or access to confidential information. A disclosed conflict is reviewed; disclosure does not automatically imply wrongdoing.

### 2.2 Gifts and hospitality

Do not accept cash gifts from vendors. Noncash gifts exceeding $100 in estimated value require disclosure to People Operations and Finance; stricter customer or anti-bribery rules take precedence.

### 2.3 Honest records

Accurately report work hours, expenses, certifications, and business results. Fabricating timecards or receipts is prohibited. Suspected fraud should be reported through approved channels.

### 2.4 Confidentiality

Respect personnel and customer privacy and follow SEC-001. Do not share internal investigation details with people who lack a legitimate need to know.

## 3. Reporting concerns

### 3.1 Reporting options

Employees may report concerns to their manager, People Operations at people@northstar.example, or the confidential ethics channel ethics@northstar.example. Employees may bypass a manager who is involved in the concern.

### 3.2 What to include

Describe what happened, approximate dates, relevant people, available records, and any immediate safety risk. Reports may be incomplete; do not delay a report while collecting evidence.

### 3.3 Immediate danger

For an imminent threat to physical safety, contact local emergency services first, then notify workplace leadership and People Operations when safe.

### 3.4 No retaliation

Northstar prohibits retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report, requests an accommodation, or participates in an investigation. Report suspected retaliation promptly even if the original allegation was not substantiated.

## 4. Review and response

### 4.1 Intake and triage

People Operations acknowledges reports when contact information is available, evaluates urgency, and assigns an impartial reviewer. A reported manager does not control the investigation.

### 4.2 Confidentiality limits

Information is shared only with those who need it to assess and address the concern, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Investigators may need to interview witnesses and preserve records.

### 4.3 Fair process

Northstar seeks relevant evidence and gives involved parties an opportunity to respond as appropriate. Outcomes may include coaching, training, corrective action, or no finding; employment action follows law and company standards.

### 4.4 Records and follow-up

Records are stored in restricted systems. The reporter may receive a general closure update, but privacy limits disclosure of another employee’s personnel action.

## 5. Common questions

### 5.1 Can I report my manager?

Yes. Contact People Operations or ethics@northstar.example directly; reporting does not require manager approval.

### 5.2 What if a colleague makes an offensive joke?

You may ask them to stop if safe and comfortable, but you are not required to confront them before reporting.

### 5.3 What if I fear retaliation?

State that concern in your report; People Operations will consider interim protections and monitor follow-up.

### 5.4 Who owns this policy?

People Operations administers HR-007. Security manages data-security incidents under SEC-001; the two teams coordinate when a case overlaps.
