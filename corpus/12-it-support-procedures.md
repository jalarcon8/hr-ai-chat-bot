# IT Support Procedures

**Northstar Technologies | Policy IT-002 | Version 1.0**

> **Fictional training document.** Northstar Technologies, its contacts, systems, and policies are invented for RAG demonstration. This document does not replace applicable law, plan documents, or written agreements.

## 1. Support channels

### 1.1 How to contact

Use the fictional IT Service Desk portal for standard requests or email helpdesk@northstar.example. Include your work contact, device asset tag if relevant, affected application, symptoms, start time, and business impact.

### 1.2 Priority levels

**P1 critical:** broad production or security-impacting outage; **P2 high:** major team disruption; **P3 normal:** individual impairment with workaround; **P4 low:** routine request or how-to question. IT determines final priority.

### 1.3 Service targets

IT aims to acknowledge P1 incidents within 15 minutes during monitored coverage, P2 within 1 business hour, P3 within 1 business day, and P4 within 2 business days. These are fictional targets, not guarantees of resolution.

## 2. Access and account recovery

### 2.1 Password reset

Use approved self-service recovery when available. If locked out, contact IT Service Desk; staff verify identity through approved methods before resetting access. Never send passwords or one-time codes to support agents.

### 2.2 MFA recovery

Report lost authenticators or unexpected prompts immediately. IT verifies identity and re-enrolls MFA through an approved workflow; Security reviews suspicious activity under SEC-001.

### 2.3 New access

Managers submit access requests stating role, application, and business need. Application owners approve privileged access where required. IT cannot grant access solely because a colleague asks in chat.

### 2.4 Offboarding

People Operations triggers the approved separation workflow; IT disables access and coordinates equipment recovery under IT-001. Employees should not self-transfer accounts.

## 3. Device and connectivity issues

### 3.1 Troubleshooting steps

Record the error, time, and steps to reproduce. Restart only when safe, check approved status notices, and avoid changing security settings. Attach screenshots without exposing passwords or sensitive customer data.

### 3.2 Lost or stolen device

Immediately notify IT and security@northstar.example. Provide asset tag, last known location, and circumstances. IT coordinates containment and replacement; do not wait for a routine ticket response.

### 3.3 Remote connectivity

If VPN or internet access fails, notify your manager about work impact and open an IT ticket. Use a managed loaner or approved alternative; do not use a household computer to bypass restrictions.

### 3.4 Hardware repair

IT decides whether to repair, replace, or loan equipment. Do not send company devices to a third-party repair shop without authorization.

## 4. Outages and escalation

### 4.1 Major outage

IT posts verified updates in the approved status channel when available. Employees should not repeatedly retry sensitive operations or promise customers restoration times without confirmed guidance.

### 4.2 Security escalation

Phishing, suspected malware, mistaken data disclosures, and unexpected MFA requests are security incidents. Report immediately to security@northstar.example or IT; SEC-001 governs incident response.

### 4.3 Ticket follow-up

Reply to the existing ticket with new information rather than opening duplicates. Escalate a business-critical blocker to your manager and IT Service Desk, citing the ticket ID.

### 4.4 Examples

**I cannot log in on day one:** follow HR-005 and request verified account recovery. **My monitor broke:** open an IT-001 equipment ticket. **I clicked a phishing link:** report immediately to Security even if nothing appears to have happened.
