# Employee Benefits Guide

**Northstar Technologies | Policy BEN-001 | Version 1.0**

> **Fictional training document.** Northstar Technologies, its contacts, systems, and policies are invented for RAG demonstration. This document does not replace applicable law, plan documents, or written agreements.

## 1. Overview and eligibility

### 1.1 Purpose and authority

This fictional guide summarizes Northstar benefits for training. Governing insurance contracts, plan documents, applicable law, and individualized enrollment notices control over this summary.

### 1.2 Eligible employees

Regular full-time US employees scheduled for 40 hours are generally eligible. Employees scheduled for 30–39 hours are evaluated by People Operations under plan and legal requirements. Contractors and agency workers are not automatically eligible.

### 1.3 Coverage start

For eligible new hires, medical, dental, and vision coverage normally starts on the first day of the month following the hire date, subject to timely enrollment and plan rules. If hired on the first day of a month, coverage normally starts that day.

## 2. Health and welfare plans

### 2.1 Medical

Northstar offers a fictional PPO option and high-deductible health plan option. Premiums, deductibles, networks, and covered services vary by plan and year; review the current plan comparison in the People Portal rather than relying on a quoted example.

### 2.2 Dental and vision

Eligible employees may elect dental and vision plans for themselves and eligible dependents. Coverage levels and annual limits are governed by the respective plan documents.

### 2.3 Life and disability

Northstar provides a basic employer-paid life insurance benefit for eligible employees and offers optional supplemental coverage. Short- and long-term disability benefits may be available subject to eligibility, waiting periods, and policy terms.

### 2.4 Employee assistance

A confidential employee assistance program offers short-term counseling and referral resources. The program is not a substitute for emergency services.

## 3. Retirement and other support

### 3.1 401(k) plan

Eligible employees may participate in the fictional Northstar 401(k) plan subject to plan eligibility and enrollment rules. Northstar’s illustrative matching formula is 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of eligible pay; vesting and contribution limits follow the governing plan.

### 3.2 Health savings account

Employees enrolled in an HSA-eligible high-deductible plan may be able to contribute to an HSA, subject to tax rules and eligibility. People Operations cannot provide individualized tax advice.

### 3.3 Wellness and learning

Optional wellness resources and approved professional development may be offered. Expenses for courses or conferences follow FIN-001 and do not automatically qualify as benefit claims.

## 4. Enrollment and changes

### 4.1 New-hire window

Eligible new hires should submit elections through People Portal → Benefits within 30 calendar days of their hire date, unless a different legally required or plan-specific deadline applies. Missing the window may delay changes until open enrollment or a qualifying event.

### 4.2 Annual open enrollment

People Operations announces dates, premiums, and plan changes before each annual open enrollment period. Elections generally apply to the following plan year, subject to plan terms.

### 4.3 Qualifying life events

Marriage, birth or adoption, loss of other coverage, and certain employment changes may permit midyear elections. Notify People Operations promptly, normally within 30 calendar days of the event, and provide required documentation. Some events have different legal deadlines.

### 4.4 Dependents

Employees must certify dependent eligibility and update coverage when a dependent no longer qualifies. Knowingly inaccurate enrollment information may result in correction or other action consistent with law.

## 5. Leave, privacy, and assistance

### 5.1 Benefits during leave

Continuation of coverage and premium payment obligations during paid or unpaid leave depend on HR-006, plan terms, and applicable law. People Operations provides individualized instructions.

### 5.2 Separation

On separation, People Operations provides notices about coverage termination and continuation rights where applicable. Do not assume all benefits end on the last workday.

### 5.3 Privacy

Medical claims and diagnoses should be shared with the insurer or designated benefits administrator, not a line manager. People Operations limits access to sensitive information.

### 5.4 Examples and contacts

**I was hired June 10; when does coverage normally begin?** July 1 if eligible and timely enrolled. **Can I change plans after having a baby?** Potentially, through the qualifying-event process. Contact people@northstar.example for plan-specific details.
